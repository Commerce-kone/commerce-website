About Us

Founded in 1978, COMMERCE began as a supplier of material handling equipment in Egypt. In 1998, we partnered with KONECRANES of Finland to deliver premium lifting technologies. Our expertise spans cranes, marine systems, industrial machinery, and more. With over 30 employees, a manufacturing facility south of Cairo, and certified production by Lloyd’s Register, we proudly serve Egypt’s industrial, governmental, and military sectors.

Our Services

  • Supplying and installing industrial cranes and lifting equipment
  • Representing international manufacturers in Egypt
  • Production of crane girders and steel structures
  • Commissioning, testing, and maintenance services
  • Supplying machinery for marine and industrial sectors
  • Handling public sector tenders and governmental contracts
